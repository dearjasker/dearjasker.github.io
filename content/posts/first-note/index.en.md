## Keep the question

Learning does not always begin with a complete answer. Sometimes a clear question is worth recording first.

- What do I already know?
- Which step remains unclear?
- Can I try a simpler example?

## Notes as a path

Give an idea a title and a question some context. A note can preserve both the conclusion and the route that led to it.
